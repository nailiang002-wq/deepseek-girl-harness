## 中文

问候现在是一张手写的蜡笔小纸条：奶油色纸面、蓝色手绘双线、小星星、浅粉涂鸦和手写字体。

- 五个时段的中文、英文问候均使用随插件内置的手写字体，离线可用，无需安装系统字体。
- 字号从 13px 调整为 18px，长句自动换行。气泡按实际文字高度定位，在窗口边缘调整位置，靠近顶部时显示在角色下方。
- 深色背景下保留清晰的纸张与文字对比；支持关闭气泡、暂停动画和系统减少动态效果。
- 字体子集约 18 KB，并随包附上字体许可证与来源。原有喂饭、铁盆帽、任务表情、拖动和问候去重继续保留。

下载 `DeepSeekGirl-Harness-v1.4.0.zip`，解压后通过 Harness 的“插件 → 添加插件”添加 `DshDeepSeekGirl` 文件夹。已安装的用户可以备份后用新版文件覆盖原插件目录，重新载入界面或退出再打开 Harness。位置、大小、帽子和问候开关沿用原来的本地设置。

验证：中文与英文五个时段、字体加载、无外部字体请求、四角与窄窗口、深色背景、关闭气泡、减少动态效果，以及原有十种动画、喂饭重播和拖动保存均通过独立浏览器测试。

## English

Greetings now arrive as handwritten crayon notes, with cream paper, blue sketch lines, a tiny star, and pale peach doodles.

The handwritten font is bundled for offline use. Both languages and all five time periods are covered, with 18px text, wrapping, placement based on measured height, and viewport-edge handling. The paper remains readable on dark backgrounds, and dismissal, pause, and reduced motion are supported. The font subset is about 18 KB; its license and provenance are included.

Download `DeepSeekGirl-Harness-v1.4.0.zip`, extract it, and add `DshDeepSeekGirl` through Harness’s Plugins → Add plugin. Existing users can back up and replace the files in their plugin folder, then reload or quit and reopen Harness. Saved preferences remain compatible.

Validated in a standalone browser: all ten greeting/language combinations, loaded offline font, no external font requests, corners, narrow-window resizing, dark backgrounds, dismissal and reduced motion. Existing animation, feeding-restart and drag-save checks also passed.
